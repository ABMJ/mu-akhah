The problem

Not every child in Jordan gets the same chance to learn. Where you live and what your family can afford change what you can reach.

Our action: pair students

A student at an international school and a student in a remote area share what they know. Each one teaches and each one learns. Families can join in and use social media to stay in touch, always with a teacher's OK.

    My journey

    From spotting a problem to taking action, measuring it and reflecting on it.

    1. 1

      Identify the problem Done

      I researched how access to learning differs across Jordan.

      • How many students are enrolled in Jordan?
      • How many students live in remote areas?
      • What share of children are not in school?
      • Why do students leave school?
    2. 2

      Take action Done

      I made Mu'akhah, an app that pairs students (and families) at international schools with students in remote areas.

    3. 3

      Measure the change

      I use a survey to see how people interact with the app, and whether they would use it.

    4. 4

      Document the journey

      I recorded a video of the process. Watch it on the right.

    5. 5

      Reflect

      After my presentation I will write about what I learned.
